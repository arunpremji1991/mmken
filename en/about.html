About Us

Who We Are & How We Help You

A closer look at MPR's story, our approach, and why organizations across Oman trust us with their brand.

Our Story

Built in Salalah, working across Oman

MPR — M'mken Public Relations — was founded to close a real gap in the Omani market: businesses needed more than advertising or social media management. They needed a partner who could shape their reputation, manage their media presence, and open doors to serious, vetted opportunities.

Today, we work across four connected disciplines — marketing, public relations, communications & media, and trusted partnerships — so our clients get one coordinated strategy instead of juggling multiple vendors.

Our Approach

Beyond social media and ad buying

Salalah and the wider Dhofar region have no shortage of agencies that can run a social page or design an ad. MPR was built to go further: we combine public relations and media strategy with the exclusive representation rights and business connections usually reserved for firms in the capital.

That means a local business can access the same caliber of brand partnerships and media reach — without having to leave Dhofar.
